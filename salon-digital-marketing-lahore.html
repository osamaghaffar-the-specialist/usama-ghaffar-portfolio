FAQ

Should a Lahore salon start with Meta Ads or Google Ads?

It depends on the service and demand. Meta is strong for visual demand generation; Google is strong when people already search for the service. Many salons can use both for different stages.

Can you track booking enquiries?

Yes. Depending on the website and process, we can track forms, calls, WhatsApp clicks or other agreed conversion actions.

Do you guarantee bookings?

No. We optimize the marketing funnel, while final bookings also depend on pricing, availability, reputation, service quality and response speed.
