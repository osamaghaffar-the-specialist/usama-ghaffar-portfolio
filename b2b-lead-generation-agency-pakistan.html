FAQ

Which channel is best for B2B leads?

It depends on buyer behavior. Search-led markets often favor Google Ads and SEO, while some categories also benefit from targeted outbound, content and remarketing.

Can you optimize for lead quality instead of volume?

Yes. Qualification fields, keyword strategy, landing-page messaging and CRM feedback can all improve the signal used for optimization.

Do you guarantee a fixed lead volume?

No. We build and optimize a measurable system based on real market response.
