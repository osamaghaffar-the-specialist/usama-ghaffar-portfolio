University & College Marketing in Lahore

Bee Marketing helps universities and colleges in Lahore generate and measure programme enquiries through Google Ads, Meta Ads, SEO, programme landing pages, conversion tracking and structured admissions follow-up.

Quick answer:

Higher-education marketing works best when every major programme has a clear path from discovery to counselling. Students researching a degree need answers about programme structure, eligibility, intake timing, campus, fee context and next steps before they submit an enquiry.

How this differs from school marketing

University and college decisions are more programme-led. Search demand can be tied to degrees, disciplines, admissions, eligibility and career intent, while Meta can build awareness before and during the intake window. That makes programme-level tracking especially important.

Programme landing pages

A strong campaign should not send every student to a generic homepage. Programme pages can explain curriculum context, eligibility, duration, campus, next intake and counselling steps while keeping the information aligned with what the institution officially offers.

Admissions CRM and source tracking

We can help structure lead capture around programme interest, intake, contact details and source so the admissions team can compare enquiry quality across campaigns. Where CRM access exists, marketing can be evaluated beyond raw lead volume.
