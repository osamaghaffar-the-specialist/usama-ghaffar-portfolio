Why PPC Underperforms

Buying more clicks does not create better demand.

Google Ads becomes expensive when campaigns chase broad traffic instead of commercial intent, clean measurement and a conversion path that matches the search.

01

Wrong search intent

Keywords look relevant on paper but attract research traffic instead of people ready to buy, book or enquire.

02

Search-term waste

Without regular negative-keyword work, budget leaks into irrelevant or low-value searches.

03

Weak conversion tracking

Campaigns optimize around incomplete or low-quality conversion actions instead of real business outcomes.

04

Landing-page mismatch

The ad promises one thing while the landing page is generic, slow or unclear, reducing conversion efficiency.

FAQ

Google Ads & PPC agency questions.

Clear answers for businesses comparing paid-search partners in Pakistan.

What does a Google Ads agency in Pakistan do?

A Google Ads agency researches search demand, builds campaign structure, manages keywords and negatives, writes ads, configures conversion tracking and optimizes bids and budgets around profitable business outcomes.

What is the difference between Google Ads and PPC?

PPC means pay-per-click advertising. Google Ads is one of the main PPC platforms and includes Search, Shopping, Performance Max, Display and other campaign types.

Do you manage Google Search Ads for lead generation?

Yes. Search campaigns can be built around high-intent commercial keywords, conversion tracking, landing-page quality and lead-quality feedback rather than raw form volume alone.

Can Google Ads work for ecommerce stores?

Yes. Ecommerce brands can use Search, Shopping and other Google campaign types to capture existing demand, with profitability evaluated through revenue, cost per purchase, ROAS and product margins.

How do you reduce wasted Google Ads spend?

Waste is reduced through search-term review, negative keywords, tighter intent matching, location and device analysis, conversion tracking, budget allocation and landing-page improvements.

How quickly can Google Ads campaigns improve?

Improvement speed depends on traffic volume, conversion volume, competition, budget and tracking quality. Early data can reveal waste quickly, while reliable scaling decisions need enough conversion evidence.
