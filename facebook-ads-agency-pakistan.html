Why Meta Ads Stall

More spend does not fix a weak acquisition system.

01

Creative fatigue

The same hooks and formats keep running after performance has already started declining.

02

Weak conversion signal

Pixel, events or attribution are incomplete, making optimization decisions less reliable.

03

Campaign fragmentation

Too many ad sets split spend and learning instead of concentrating signal on the strongest opportunities.

04

Scaling without economics

ROAS can look acceptable while margins, refunds, shipping or repeat-purchase economics make the growth unprofitable.

FAQ

Facebook & Meta Ads agency questions.

Clear answers for brands comparing paid social partners in Pakistan.

What does a Facebook Ads agency in Pakistan do?

A Facebook Ads agency plans campaign structure, audiences, offers, creative testing, tracking, optimization and budget allocation to help a business acquire customers more efficiently through Meta platforms.

Do you manage Instagram Ads as well as Facebook Ads?

Yes. Facebook and Instagram placements are managed through Meta Ads Manager as part of one paid social strategy, with placement and creative decisions based on performance data.

Can you improve an ecommerce brand's Meta Ads ROAS?

ROAS improvement can involve campaign structure, creative testing, offer positioning, product-page conversion, tracking quality, audience signals and budget allocation. Results depend on the store economics, offer and market.

How do you decide when to scale a Meta Ads campaign?

Scaling decisions are based on purchase volume, cost per purchase, ROAS, contribution margin, creative stability and whether performance remains efficient as spend increases.

Do you work with new ad accounts?

Yes. New accounts can be structured around clean tracking, clear conversion events, focused campaign architecture and a testing plan before aggressive scaling.

What do you need before starting Facebook Ads management?

Typical requirements include access to the Meta Business portfolio and ad account, website or store, pixel and conversion tracking, product or service details, current creative assets, margins and business goals.
