Before You Send

Useful answers, without the back-and-forth.

A strong first message does not need to be long. It only needs enough context to make the next conversation productive.

Can I get in touch without a finished brief?
Yes. Share the objective, the current obstacle and anything you already know. A rough but honest picture is more useful than a polished document with missing context.
Do you take on focused, single-service projects?
A focused engagement can make sense when one clear bottleneck needs specialist attention. If the issue connects multiple parts of the journey, the recommended scope may reflect that.
What should I include in the first message?
Include what you want to improve, what is happening now, where you think the constraint is and any timing or system context that could influence the work.
Can we begin through email or LinkedIn?
Yes. Use whichever channel is easiest. The project form simply prepares the essential context so the first exchange can begin with more clarity.
