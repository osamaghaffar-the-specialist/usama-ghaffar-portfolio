Admissions landing pages

A focused school admissions page should make the parent journey easier. It can explain grade levels, curriculum, campus, application process, open-day details, fee context where appropriate, transport or facilities information and a clear enquiry action. We can connect campaigns to dedicated pages rather than sending every visitor to a generic homepage.

Parent enquiry quality

Forms can capture grade level, preferred campus, admission year or term and contact details without becoming too long. Where a school has multiple branches, campaign structure and reporting can separate campus demand so the admissions team understands which locations and messages generate useful enquiries.

Google Business Profile and local trust

Parents often compare schools through maps, reviews, websites and brand searches. Local SEO, accurate business information, useful photos and consistent campus information can support trust alongside paid campaigns. Our SEO agency Lahore work can support the wider search system.

When should a school start admissions marketing?

Planning before the admission window gives more time to build awareness, test messages and improve landing pages before the highest-intent period begins.

Can you market a new school campus?

Yes. A launch plan can combine local awareness, search demand, campus pages, parent enquiries and open-day campaigns.

Can we track leads by campus?

Yes, where forms, campaign structure and reporting are configured to capture campus or location information.
