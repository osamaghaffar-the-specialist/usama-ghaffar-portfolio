Quick answer:

Restaurant marketing should start with the business goal. Dine-in traffic, delivery orders, reservations, catering enquiries and brand awareness need different campaigns. FAQ

What should a Lahore restaurant advertise first?

Start with the business objective and a strong offer or differentiator. Then choose the channel where that demand is easiest to capture or create.

Can you help with local SEO and paid ads together?

Yes. The two can work together: paid ads capture demand immediately while SEO and local visibility build a longer-term discovery channel.

Do you guarantee orders or footfall?

No. We optimize measurable marketing inputs and conversions, while actual orders and footfall depend on many business factors.
