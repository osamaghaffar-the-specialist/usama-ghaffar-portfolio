FAQ

Can law firms advertise on Google?

Campaign suitability depends on the firm's services and applicable platform or professional rules. We structure campaigns around truthful service claims and measurable enquiry intent.

Should each legal service have its own page?

Usually yes when the firm genuinely offers distinct services and users search for them differently. Each page should be useful, specific and non-duplicative.

Do you guarantee signed clients?

No. We optimize visibility and enquiry systems, while signed clients depend on factors beyond marketing.
